templeHistory: |
  Centuries ago, venerable Siddha masters chose this secluded grove of Jhadheena for intense dhyana (meditation) and tapasya. The ground was consecrated by the eternal sound of Om and sacred havan offerings, making it one of the most spiritually potent vortices in the Jhajjar region.

  The revered Baba Sidhnath blessed this holy ashram with his divine physical presence, showering unceasing compassion, healing spiritual afflictions, and establishing the unbroken tradition of free Annakshetra (langar) for all seekers regardless of caste, creed, or background.

  The monumental ancient tree whose massive gnarled branches spread across the ashram sky was consecrated during his time. Devotees tie sacred mauli threads and perform parikrama to fulfill spiritual vows — a tradition observed to this day.

  The tranquil white marble courtyard and inner sanctum were crafted by devoted pilgrims, housing the sacred dhuni, shivling, and holy shrines. The grand ceremonial archway was adorned in deep celestial navy with opulent 24K gold filigree carvings, suspending the resonant Ashta-dhatu brass temple bell that purifies the atmosphere with each ring.
aboutTemple: |
  Shree Baba Sidhnath Mandir stands as a living sanctuary of ancient Siddha tradition. The ashram is open to all pilgrims and seekers throughout the year, offering free prasad, resting facilities under the shade of ancient trees, and a deeply meditative environment away from the noise of modern life.

  The temple complex includes the main sanctum housing the sacred Shivling, the ancient Mahavriksha (sacred tree), the white marble mandapa, the Yajna Shala (fire ceremony pavilion), consecrated samadhi shrines, and the iconic Royal Navy & Gold archway that marks the entrance to the divine realm.

  All festivals are celebrated with full Vedic rituals, free mahaprasad distribution, and devotional music. The spirit of Baba Sidhnath Ji — unconditional service, compassion, and divine grace — continues to guide this sacred space.
